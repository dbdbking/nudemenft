Reporting a problem

To help us understand what happened, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The NU__MEN app version, if known, and the mode you were using.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.

A screenshot is optional. Please remove personal information and never send passwords or payment details.

Quick help

How do I change modes or appearance?

Tap the menu button in the corner of the app. Choose Analog Clock, Digital Clock, Drawing, or Voting. Use the sliders to change the character color, size, obedience, and background color.

How do I draw or scatter the characters?

Select Drawing mode and drag your finger across the screen. Shake your device to scatter the characters. In clock modes, they will return to form the time.

What if the app stops updating?

Close and reopen NU__MEN. If the problem continues, email us with your device details and the steps that caused it.

02 / Your data

Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026

NU__MEN does not collect or transmit personal data through the app.

This policy covers NU__MEN for iPhone and iPad, created by Francis Lam / db-db-db, and support provided through the email address on this page.

Information in the app

The app works without an account. It contains no advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs. It does not send your activity or preferences to the developer.

Preferences stored on your device

NU__MEN saves your selected mode and appearance settings using the device’s standard preferences storage. These settings are used only to remember your choices. Deleting the app removes its local preferences. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Device permissions

NU__MEN does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.

If you contact support

If you email dbdbking@gmail.com, you voluntarily provide your email address, message, and any attachments. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store this correspondence.

Support messages may remain in our email history after your request is resolved. To request deletion of your correspondence, contact the same address. There is no app account or developer-held app activity data to delete.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age. Please avoid including a child’s personal information in support messages.

This webpage

This page includes no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or external embeds. The website hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the page.

Updates and questions

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact Francis Lam / db-db-db at dbdbking@gmail.com.
