01 / Help

Support

For gameplay questions, purchase help, and bug reports.

Get in touch

dbdbking@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, TEGO version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Please don’t send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I move and rotate a piece?

Touch or drag across the board to choose a column. Swipe up to rotate the falling piece, or swipe down to drop it faster. You can also use the movement, drop, and rotation controls beneath the board.

Where can I find the rules?

Tap the pause button at the bottom centre of the game. Use the arrow on the right to open the illustrated rules, then the left and right arrows to turn the pages. Tap the bottom-centre button to return to your game.

What does the Upgrade Pack unlock?

The Upgrade Pack unlocks Endless Mode. Existing purchases remain valid. Advertising has been removed for everyone from version 1.1, whether or not you own the Upgrade Pack.

How do I restore an existing purchase?

Pause the game and choose Restore on the upgrade or quit menu. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoring a purchase does not buy it again. If restoration fails, email us with the message shown in the app.

The game closes or something looks wrong.

Close and reopen TEGO, then check the App Store for an available update. If the problem continues, contact us with your device and app details. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step, because your saved game and local scores may be removed.

How do the leaderboards work?

TEGO uses Apple Game Center to submit scores and show leaderboards. Manage your Game Center account in your device’s Settings. No separate account with us is needed to play.

I have a billing or refund question.

Apple handles in-app purchase billing. Review a transaction or request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem service. We do not receive your payment-card details.

02 / Your data

Privacy policy

For TEGO version 1.1 and later, and this support page.

Effective and last updated: November 3, 2026
Publisher: Francis Lam / db-db-db

Information in the game

TEGO version 1.1 and later contain no advertising, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. It does not require an account with us. We do not operate a game-data server or player-account database.

The game uses Apple’s Game Center and in-app purchase services. Those features exchange information with Apple as described below.

Data stored on your device

TEGO saves your local scores, current game, game settings, and whether you own the Upgrade Pack. This information lets you resume play and retain your purchase benefits. It is not uploaded to a server operated by us.

Deleting the app normally removes its local data. Device backups, purchase records, and Game Center records may remain separately under your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Apple Game Center

When you are signed in to Game Center, TEGO authenticates through Apple and submits scores to its leaderboards. Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible to other players. We do not maintain a separate player database or send Game Center information to our own server.

Apple manages Game Center accounts and records. Read Game Center & Privacy and use your device’s Game Center settings to manage your account and sharing choices.

In-app purchases

Apple processes the optional Upgrade Pack. The game receives transaction results and stores your purchase entitlement locally. We do not receive payment-card details or send purchase receipts to a developer-operated server.

Apple manages purchase records, billing, refunds, and restoration under its terms and privacy policy.

Support email

If you email dbdbking@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store this correspondence.

Support messages may remain in our email history after your request is resolved. You can request deletion of your correspondence at the same address. Please send only information relevant to your request.

This webpage

This page has no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by its code, or external embeds. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to serve and secure the page. Read GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

External links open services with their own privacy policies. Opening an email link does not send a message until you choose to send it.

Your choices

We do not sell personal information or share game data with advertising or analytics networks. You can remove local game data by deleting the app, manage Apple Account and Game Center information through Apple, and contact us about support correspondence. There is no separate developer-held game account to delete.

Updates and contact

If the app’s information practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. For support or privacy questions, contact Francis Lam / db-db-db at dbdbking@gmail.com.
