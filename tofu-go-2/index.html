Support

No developer account or login is needed to play.

Get in touch

dbdbking@gmail.com

For a bug, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please don’t send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I restore my Upgrade Pack?

Open the game’s Options menu and choose Restore Upgrade. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Restoring an existing purchase does not buy it again. If it is not restored, email us with the message shown in the app.

What does the Upgrade Pack include?

The Upgrade Pack provides three tofu and three tofu balls. Existing purchases remain valid. From version 1.1, everyone gets ad-free gameplay, whether or not they own the Upgrade Pack.

The game closes or something looks wrong.

Close and reopen the app, then check the App Store for an available update. If it happens again, tell us what you were doing and which device and app version you use. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot, because local game progress may be removed.

How does Game Center work?

Apple Game Center handles leaderboards and achievements. Manage your Game Center account in your device’s Settings. The game has no separate db-db-db login. If a leaderboard or achievement is not updating, include that detail in your support email.

I have a billing or refund question.

Apple handles in-app purchase billing. You can review a transaction or request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem service. We cannot access your payment-card details.

Privacy policy

For Tofu Go! 2 version 1.1 and later, and this support page.

Effective and last updated: November 3, 2026
Publisher: Francis Lam / db-db-db

1. The short version

Tofu Go! 2 is a single-player game. Version 1.1 and later contain no advertising, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. The game does not request permission to track you and does not require an account with us. We do not operate a game-data server or player-account database.

The game uses Apple’s Game Center and in-app purchase services. Those features exchange information with Apple as described below.

2. Information on your device

The game stores settings, local scores and progress, and whether you own the Upgrade Pack. It uses this information to run the game and remember your preferences and purchase benefits. It does not upload this local information to a server operated by us.

Local data remains until it is changed or removed. Deleting the app normally removes its local data. Device backups and Apple-managed purchase or Game Center records may remain separately.

3. Apple Game Center

When Game Center is enabled, the game authenticates through Apple and submits scores and achievements. Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible to other players under Apple’s Game Center settings. We do not maintain a separate player database or send Game Center information to our own server.

Apple manages these accounts and records. See Game Center & Privacy and use your device’s Game Center settings to manage your account and sharing choices.

4. Purchases

Apple processes the optional Upgrade Pack through StoreKit. The game receives transaction results and stores a local purchase-entitlement flag. We do not receive your payment-card details or send purchase receipts to a developer-operated server.

Apple manages purchase records, billing, refunds, and restoration under its own terms and privacy policy. The game is ad-free regardless of purchase status.

5. Support email

If you email dbdbking@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve the issue. Email is handled by our email provider; this is separate from data stored inside the game.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed for your request, related follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence using the email address above. Please send only information relevant to your request.

6. This website and external links

This page has no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by its code, or embedded third-party media. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser details, and request time, to provide and protect its hosting service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Links may open other websites or apps. Those destinations handle information under their own privacy policies. Opening an email link does not send a message until you choose to send it.

7. Sharing and your choices

We do not sell personal information or share game data with advertising or analytics networks. Apple and our email and website providers handle information to provide their respective services. They may process it in countries other than where you live, under their own privacy practices.

You can delete local game data by deleting the app, manage Game Center and Apple Account information through Apple, and contact us about support correspondence. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information or object to its use. Email us with your request; we can explain what we hold and direct requests about Apple or GitHub records to the relevant provider.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or our information practices change. We will publish the current policy here and revise the date above.
